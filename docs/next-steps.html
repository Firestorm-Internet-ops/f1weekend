Step 1 · Now

Measure the site

Today we can't see who visits or who clicks "Book". Google shows only 18 clicks from February to August 2026.

✓ Code done:

  • Ahrefs Web Analytics tracker added to every page.
  • Every Book click sends a book_click event to Ahrefs (and to Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics when they're on).
  • Book buttons now open the partner site reliably. Before, Safari and mobile browsers could block them as popups.

Your part:

  1. Merge and deploy the change.
  2. After a day, open Ahrefs → Web Analytics → F1weekend and check that visits appear.
  3. Click a Book button yourself and check that a book_click event shows up.
  4. Let it collect data for 2–4 weeks.
